About TailRef

This application is a free, source-available drawing, reference and 3D posing tool, built by an unemployed artist using various vibe-coding tools.

A Note from the Creator

First of all, have fun!

TailRef is a little project that grew out of my own needs as an artist. I hope it becomes useful to you, too.

If there's a feature you'd like to see, or something you think could be better, feel free to reach out to me via email. I'm always happy to hear your ideas.

And here's something I'd really love to do: If you create something using TailRef, send it my way! With your permission, I'd love to feature your artwork on the application's startup screen, with your name credited alongside it. I'd like every launch of TailRef to feel a little like visiting a community gallery.

Take care of yourselves, and keep creating.

A Little Belief

AI should be a tool in an artist's hands, never a replacement for the artist.

3D POSE — FIRST TIME?

Posing a 3D character is easier than it looks. You pose it, you look from the angle you like, and you send it to the canvas. That's all.

1
Show the joint circles In the POSE card, turn Controls on. A small circle appears on each joint: shoulder, elbow, knee and so on. You pose the character by grabbing these circles.
2
Grab a circle and drag Press a circle and drag the mouse. The joint turns the way you drag: pull the shoulder circle up and the arm rises. If you make a mistake, Ctrl+Z takes it back.
3
Turn the camera, not the character Alt + drag rotates around the character. Alt + middle drag slides the view. The wheel zooms. If you lose the character, press F (or ⌖ Focus) and it comes back to the middle. Blender user? Middle-drag rotates. You can change this in Settings → 3D Pose.
4
Save the pose you like In the SAVE card, press ○ Save. You can give it a name first. Click a saved pose later and the character returns to exactly that pose.
5
Send it to the canvas In the TOOLS card, press ◉ Capture (Ctrl+Shift+S also works). Choose a size, check it with Preview render, then press Add to board. The pose becomes an image on your board, ready to draw over.
If something gets stuck
  • Nothing moves when I drag — Controls may be off, or the wrong tool is selected. Press E for rotate, W for move.
  • The character is gone — press F (or ⌖ Focus) and it comes back to the middle.
  • The pose is a mess — press ⟲ Reset in the POSE card.
  • The panel closed — click 3D at the top to open it again.

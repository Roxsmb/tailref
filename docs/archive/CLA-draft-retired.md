## Why this document exists

TailRef is distributed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and may
also be offered under separate commercial terms by the copyright holder. To keep
that option available, the project needs permission from contributors to use,
modify, redistribute and commercially license their contributions.

This draft is intentionally explicit about those rights. It is also written so
that contributors **retain copyright** in their work.

## 1. Parties

- **"You"** — the individual or legal entity submitting a contribution.
- **"Maintainer"** — Emre Melik Nameli, the copyright holder of TailRef, or a
  successor entity designated in writing.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Project"** — the TailRef software and its associated repositories,
  documentation and assets.
- **"Contribution"** — any original work of authorship that you intentionally
  submit to the Project, including code, documentation, tests, translations,
  designs and assets, in any form and through any channel (pull request, patch,
  issue attachment, email).
- **"Submit"** — to send, upload, post or otherwise make available to the
  Project or the Maintainer.

## 3. Copyright

You **retain copyright ownership** of your Contribution. Nothing in this
agreement transfers or assigns your copyright to the Maintainer.

## 4. Copyright license grant

You grant the Maintainer a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable** copyright license to:

1. use, reproduce, modify and prepare derivative works of your Contribution;
2. publicly display, publicly perform and distribute your Contribution and

3. sublicense and redistribute your Contribution, in source or binary form,

   including commercial license terms;
4. combine your Contribution with other works and license the result.

This grant includes the right to relicense your Contribution for commercial
purposes. It does not oblige the Maintainer to use your Contribution.

You grant the Maintainer and recipients of the Project a perpetual, worldwide,

offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer the Project, where that

If you institute patent litigation alleging that the Project or a Contribution
infringes a patent, the patent license granted here for that work terminates as
of the date the litigation is filed.

## 6. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you waive and agree not to assert
moral rights in your Contribution against the Maintainer, the Project or its
licensees. Where waiver is not permitted, you agree not to assert those rights
in a way that interferes with the licenses granted here.

## 7. Representations

You represent that:

1. each Contribution is your original work, or you have sufficient rights to
   submit it under this agreement;
2. you have the legal authority to grant the licenses in this agreement (for

3. your Contribution does not, to your knowledge, infringe third-party
   copyrights, patents, trademarks or other rights;
4. if your Contribution includes third-party material, you have identified it
   and its license, and it is compatible with this agreement;
5. your Contribution contains no confidential information, secrets or
   credentials.

## 8. No warranty

Unless required by applicable law or agreed to in writing, you provide your
Contribution **"as is"**, without warranties or conditions of any kind, express
or implied.

## 9. No obligation

The Maintainer is not obliged to accept, merge, publish or maintain any
Contribution, and may remove a Contribution at any time.

### Review checklist for counsel

- Confirm the copyright and patent grants are enforceable in the maintainer's
  jurisdiction and in the contributors' likely jurisdictions.
- Confirm the moral-rights waiver is valid, or replace it with a consent that
  works locally.
- Confirm whether an employer sign-off is needed for contributions made during
  employment.
- Decide how acceptance is recorded (CLA bot, signed file, email).
- Add governing law, jurisdiction and a contact address.
- Confirm the agreement is compatible with the PolyForm Noncommercial License
  and with any commercial license the project offers.
